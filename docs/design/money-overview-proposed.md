# Money Overview — proposed, awaiting approval

One screen only. Purpose: explain where work earnings went, what remains before personal tax, and whether a like-for-like contractor payout agrees with the statement. No charts, receipt scanning, invoice generation or Pro sales card in Overview.

Hierarchy: Money header with amount visibility toggle/profile; Overview/Expenses/Insights tabs; pay period selector from driver configuration; records waterfall; payout comparison. Mobile 16px side gutters, 16px card padding, 12–16px gaps, 48px CTA and controls, auto-height cards. Desktop max width 1120px with waterfall left and comparison right. Money selected in the existing five-item nav. Distinct borders and readable text in both themes; status always text plus icon, never colour only.

Example closed period 17 Aug–13 Sep 2026:
Earnings £5,499.55; configured franchise fee 6% −£329.97; contractor charges −£905.46; my expenses −£516.49; estimated take-home £3,747.63. Primary financial emphasis is take-home, with “Before personal tax · Based on recorded costs”. Breakdown row labels open existing relevant records; per-day/rate formula and individual costs stay behind “View calculation”. Unrecorded expected recurring costs, when any, show a link “Expected costs not yet recorded” and are not silently added to or removed from recorded totals.

Comparison scope: expected contractor payout £4,264.12 (earnings minus contractor fee and charges) vs statement payout £4,217.69; difference −£46.43, labelled “Statement is £46.43 lower”. Personal expenses are excluded from BOTH sides of this comparison. Same pay period, currency, tax/VAT treatment and payout scope required; otherwise show “Complete the comparison” and no definitive discrepancy. A negative difference is not proof of underpayment. The example implies comparison complete but unresolved, not reconciled or paid.

Primary button changes with lifecycle: tracking/awaiting statement -> Check pay (manual free); statement received -> Review statement; checked difference -> Review difference; reconciled -> View check. Always opens the selected period. Do not offer one-tap reconciliation without matching reviewed data. Status progression follows existing tracking/awaiting/received/checked/reconciled contract; edited records trigger Needs recheck and keep old check visible as stale. Reconciled means agreement was confirmed, not money received or personal taxes paid. My expenses remain driver records, even when payout reconciles. Label estimate/reconciled adjacent to the exact applicable value, not the entire page.

First use: choose/set pay structure and period if missing; no fabricated earnings or zero-for-unknown fee. No records -> “No work recorded this period” and Log work. Returning user: current pay period unless Home explicitly links another range; carry selection into Expenses and Check Pay. Future forecast does not blend with recorded work.

Loading: stable skeletons, no £0 flashes. Partial data: show known work earnings, hide affected net/comparison, say which records unavailable and offer Retry. Offline: cached-as-of timestamp, static known values, no automatic checked/reconciled state changes. Show “Connect to refresh” and keep period navigation working for cached periods. Errors do not erase good cached data. Unknown fee or incomplete historical pay config blocks misleading net total.

Interactions: tap feedback, 200ms panel/expansion changes; no rolling money counters, celebratory animations or delayed saves. Reduced motion immediate. iOS/PWA/Capacitor safe areas and bottom bar spacing, 16px inputs, scroll/focus management for period picker. Android Back closes picker/detail before leaving. Desktop mouse/keyboard focus, two columns, no stretched mobile card.

Analytics: consent-gated money_overview_viewed, money_period_changed, money_breakdown_opened, money_comparison_opened. Enum lifecycle/action/source only, no dates, amounts, records, names or contractor identifiers. No replay/autocapture. Navigation-derived event data does not require saving a financial summary.

Data/privacy: derive totals deterministically in integer pennies from saved work/rate snapshots and source-separated expenses. Preserve established rounding policy; never AI arithmetic. Do not persist duplicate totals on page view. Statement results retained only after reviewed confirmation. No extra uploads, AI or API calls from Overview; existing listeners can be reused. Money visibility toggle masks every monetary value, including discrepancy copy and expanded rows. No sensitive screen content in analytics.

Before implementation: confirm comparison scope in Check Pay's internal model and reconcile existing periodRecord status field names; verify source completeness and snapshot rounding; resolve VAT/inclusive/exclusive mismatches explicitly rather than converting silently. Approval requested for this layout and like-for-like comparison; no Money Overview implementation in this commit.
